Services

Three ways I work with clients, each on a fractional basis.

  • Product and market leadership

    Deciding what to build or buy next is hard when the market moves this fast. I advise product teams, insurers and investors on where AI agents and automation fit, what to build or buy next, and what it takes to run them in a regulated environment.

    Ask about product and market leadership

  • Pre-sales and solutions leadership

    Deals stall between a good demo and a buyer who can explain the value internally. I tie the product to a measurable outcome, shape proposals and statements of work, and scope pilots that prove the case.

    Ask about pre-sales and solutions leadership

  • Customer leadership

    Founder-led customer management stops working as the customer list grows. I lead customer success, delivery and account growth, set up how customers are onboarded, measured and retained, and step in on the accounts that matter most.

    Ask about customer leadership

Who I work with

AI and automation vendors

Software companies that need senior product, pre-sales or customer leadership before a full-time hire makes sense.

Insurers and enterprises

Organisations adopting AI agents that want someone who has delivered them to test the plan and the suppliers.

Investors and boards

Funds and boards that need an informed view of a product, a market or a team.

Expertise
